Cook Time: 15

Serves: 4

### Ingredients
* 1 TJs herbed pizza dough
* 2/3 TJs jar classic pizza sauce
* 8oz (50g) buffalo mozzarella, torn into small pieces (or 1/2 bag TJ's shredded mozzarella
* 2 cups ricotta
* 1 cup, halved cherry tomatoes
* Fresh basil leaves
* Salt and pepper, to taste

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450F.
2. Roll out pizza dough to fit a 13x18" sheet pan
3. Generously spoon pizza sauce and top with mozzarella
4. Spread out tomatoes on the pizza
5. Scoop generous dollops of ricotta spread out throughout the pizza.
6. Bake in the oven for approximately 10-12 minutes, until cheese is bubbly and crust is slightly golden. If the top is a bit watery, just take a paper towel to dab it.
7. Top with basil!

Note: if you want a more meat-y version, you can put cooked sausage instead of ricotta! Also delicious.
