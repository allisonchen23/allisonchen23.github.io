Prep Time: 20

Cook Time: 15

Serves: 4

{% include difficulty.html difficulty_level=3 %}

*Recipe adapted from [What's Gaby Cooking](https://whatsgabycooking.com/caramelized-wild-mushroom-pizza/#recipeJump)*

---

### Ingredients
#### For the wild mushrooms
* 1 lb wild mushrooms
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* salt

#### For the Pizza
* ½ pound pizza dough
* 2 tablespoons olive oil (or truffle-infused oil!)
* 2 cloves garlic finely chopped
* 1 1/2 cup fresh mozzarella
* 3/4 cup shredded fontina cheese
* 2 cup caramelized wild mushrooms from above
* 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese
* Fresh thyme leaves (optional)
* 1-2 cups arugula
* Flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper

### Instructions
#### For the mushrooms
1. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, for 8 to 10 minutes until golden and caramelized. Season with salt. Drain any excess liquid if needed. Set aside.

#### For the pizza
1. Preheat oven to 450F.
2. Roll out pizza dough to fit a 13x18" sheet pan. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon olive oil and bake for 8 minutes.
3. Remove the crust from the oven. Scatter the garlic, mozzarella, fontina, and caramelized mushrooms evenly over the top.
4. Return the pizza to the oven and bake for 6 to 10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling and the crust is golden.
5. Remove from the oven, sprinkle with crumbled goat cheese, fresh thyme leaves, flaky salt, and black pepper, and arugula. Slice and serve immediately.
