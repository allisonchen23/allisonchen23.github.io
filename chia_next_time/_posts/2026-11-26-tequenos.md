### Ingredients
#### Dough
* 2 eggs
* 60 g of butter
* 500 g of flour
* water

####

* 450 g of frying cheese (queso de freir)

### Instructions
1. Mix the flour, the eggs, and the butter in a big bowl, and then add water little by little until you get a dough that is not too sticky but it clumps together.
2. Break the dough into two pieces and roll the dough out into a thin layer, thinner than a pizza crust.
3. Use a sharp knife to cut thin strands of dough, about 1 inch wide.
4. Cut block of cheese into 48 pieces (thirds along the length, half then 8 pieces)
5. Wrap each cheese stick in the doughs
6. Fry!
